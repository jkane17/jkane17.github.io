Quill

A terminal IDE for Q: edit code, query running processes, and explore results without leaving your terminal.

Jump to downloads ↓ Getting started ↓

What you can do

  • Edit Q: syntax highlighting, tabs, and search and replace.
  • Run queries: start a Q REPL or connect to an existing process.
  • Explore results: browse tables, plot graphs, and export data.
  • Find help: built-in Q reference, guides, and a command palette.

Getting started

Use a terminal on Linux, macOS, or Windows through WSL. Bring your own licensed Q installation; Quill does not include Q or kdb+.

  1. Download the archive for your platform above.
  2. Extract it and launch Quill. Replace VERSION and TARGET below with the values in your downloaded filename. 
    tar xzf quill-VERSION-TARGET.tar.gz
./quill-VERSION-TARGET/quill
  3. Press Ctrl+H for shortcuts, or F4 to browse the built-in documentation.

On macOS, downloaded archives may be quarantined. See the download guide for macOS instructions and archive checksums.
